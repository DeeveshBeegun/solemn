# Solemn threat model

Week 11 covers a local paper worker, its PostgreSQL database, read-only market and RPC providers, and the Jupiter quote client. PostgreSQL and the local operator account are trusted for state integrity and administration. Remote provider data is untrusted. A database superuser, compromised host or malicious deployment can bypass process-level controls. This is a paper system and these controls do not protect live capital.

| Threat                                 | Impact and likelihood                                               | Mitigation and detection                                                                                                                                            | Recovery and residual risk                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Compromised provider response          | Bad quotes or marks, plausible                                      | Validate schemas, mints, units, quote age, route and price limits. Separate provider data from booked paper fills. Watch provider errors and disagreements.         | Quarantine the source and compare independent observations. A plausible forged response can pass structural checks.                             |
| Malicious token metadata               | Misleading identity or security decisions, plausible                | Use mint IDs and verified on-chain risk evidence. Treat display metadata as untrusted. Reject stale or conflicting assessments.                                     | Reassess the mint and discard unauthenticated metadata. A token's economic risk remains.                                                        |
| Unexpected swap instructions           | Unintended transfers if a future signer accepted them, material     | Inspect signer, payer, version, account roles and recognized instructions. Block unsupported routes and lookup tables. No signer is connected.                      | Quarantine the transaction and review the instruction and account data. Current inspection cannot establish downstream CPI effects.             |
| Credential disclosure                  | Provider misuse or database access, plausible                       | Keep credentials in ignored configuration, send headers only to intended providers, restrict redirects, and redact headers and errors.                              | Rotate credentials, invalidate sessions and review provider and database logs. External logs and host compromise remain risks.                  |
| Unauthorized configuration change      | Unsafe startup or misleading data, plausible                        | Require paper mode and reject known signing or broadcasting settings. Freeze forward policy and replay fingerprint.                                                 | Restore reviewed configuration and restart into reconciliation. Host administrators can replace the executable.                                 |
| Duplicate order processing             | Double booking or oversell, plausible during retries                | Session ownership, stable order IDs, unique journal rows and transactional checkpoints. Audit ownership conflicts.                                                  | Halt and compare orders, fills and the journal frontier. Privileged out-of-band writes can bypass the application.                              |
| Database corruption                    | Unreliable balances and exposure, lower likelihood with high impact | Verify journal hashes and replay materialized records. Block forward writes on frontier mismatch.                                                                   | Preserve evidence, restore a verified copy and reconcile before requesting a reset. A privileged attacker could alter both evidence and hashes. |
| Stale signals or market observations   | Orders based on obsolete conditions, common in outages              | Gate on observed timestamps, executable quote and complete candle history. Reject stale intents and block the final entry gate.                                     | Restore reliable observations and rebuild coverage. Freshness does not guarantee fill quality.                                                  |
| WebSocket disconnect or missing events | Data gap and unpriced positions, plausible                          | Record gaps, invalidate pending entries and mark unreliable valuations. Reconnection alone does not qualify as coverage.                                            | Recover verified data and a complete indicator window. Unobservable trades may never be reconstructed.                                          |
| Liquidity disappearance                | Failed exit or severe modeled loss, plausible                       | Validate reserves, supported execution model and fresh quote. Retain blocked positions and alert.                                                                   | Reassess pool state and retry only when supported. An unsellable holding can lose its full value.                                               |
| RPC outage or throttling               | Risk evidence, simulation or health unavailable, plausible          | Bounded HTTP reads and retry delays. Quote or simulation failure cannot cause a paper fill.                                                                         | Confirm provider recovery, refresh evidence and retain uncertainty during gaps. Alternative endpoints can disagree.                             |
| Failed stop-loss                       | Position remains exposed, plausible                                 | Record the outcome and retain quantity and cost basis. Surface unresolved exits and prevent new entries while valuation is uncertain.                               | Use fresh executable evidence for protective attempts. No guaranteed stop price.                                                                |
| Worker ownership conflict              | Competing writes or liveness loss, plausible during deployment      | Dedicated session lock, persisted owner PID, same-session write verification and account-row serialization.                                                         | Stop the contender, validate the owner and inspect the incident. A conflict before an account exists can be logged locally only.                |
| Incorrect arithmetic                   | Incorrect risk limits or ledger state, plausible regression         | Exact decimal and integer math, fill and posting invariants, replay reconciliation and tests.                                                                       | Reproduce from immutable inputs and investigate mismatches before reset. Model assumptions remain separate from arithmetic correctness.         |
| Unbounded retries or event load        | Quota exhaustion or stalled worker, plausible                       | Bounded HTTP attempts, bounded queues, fixed timeouts, capped read reports and operational impairment states.                                                       | Drain or stop safely, inspect queue and rate-limit counters and restore evidence. The current quota spacing is per process.                     |
| Unauthorized administrative action     | Denial of service or unauthorized recovery, plausible               | Local CLI and database credentials, OS actor in the audit, explicit halt and reset reasons, transactional health and ownership checks. No remote control endpoint.  | Revoke access, preserve audit history and revalidate controls. The OS actor is a local attribution field and depends on host access discipline. |
| Accidental live activation             | Real financial loss, severe                                         | Compiled-in paper path, validated mode, no private-key handling or submission API. Jupiter client has a fixed order path and simulation uses read-only RPC methods. | Stop workers and isolate credentials and network access immediately. Review the deployed artifact before restarting.                            |

## Week 12 boundary review

The Tokens V2 client now validates its origin before making requests and withholds credentials on loopback. Risk and simulation RPC reject known swap hosts and issue fixed read-only method names. Discovery-only sockets omit credentials. The [issue register](../release/week12-issues.md) records the fixes and remaining assumptions. The compiled worker's release smoke checks unsafe configuration rejection.
